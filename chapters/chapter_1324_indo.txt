Bab 1323: Entropy Reversal and Rejuvenation

"Oh? Please elaborate." Li Fan's expression turned serious."Can Fellow Daoist discern the miraculous use of this True Immortal seal script character, [Ancient]?" Zhong Daogong deliberately kept him in suspense. Li Fan pondered for a moment, tracing lightly through the air with his finger."Ancient means the past, the old...""...Time reversal?"As he carefully examined the mysterious character on the ruins below, Li Fan's eyes suddenly lit up.

Zhong Daogong clapped his hands in praise."Fellow Daoist's comprehension is truly extraordinary. Even the elders of our Immortal Ark require several days before gaining any insight from it. Yet you managed to see through it at a glance!""However, describing its power as time reversal may not be entirely accurate.

All things would evolve backward toward their former selves. From life to death, reduced to drifting sand. Truly becoming ancient and passing away..." Zhong Daogong waved his hand, causing dozens of scenes to appear before them. The moment the [Ancient] character manifested, cultivators, towering trees, endless buildings across the land, and even the world itself began evolving backward as though time had reversed.

In the end, everything transformed into microscopic particles and was scattered by the wind."If this original inscription still retained significant power, then no matter how tempting it might be, our Immortal Ark would only dare avoid it and never covet it.

But now..."The Immortal Ark cultivators then demonstrated the practical use of the True Immortal seal script. The oldest among the elders, cultivator Jian Daosheng of the Severed Immortal Tower, slowly flew toward the [Ancient] character. He did not approach too closely and instead hovered in the air.

The power of the [Ancient] character had supposedly been exhausted, as Zhong Daogong had said, but that was only relative to its former state. In truth, a faint residue of its power still remained within the original inscription. Compared to its peak strength, it was negligible.

Yet just like the [Dawn] character, overwhelming power brought disaster while weaker power could bring benefits. Once the reversing force of the [Ancient] character had diminished to an appropriate level...The wrinkles on Jian Daosheng's face gradually faded.

Black strands appeared amidst his white brows and hair. His bent body straightened once more, and even his spirit became noticeably more vigorous. This scene was unmistakably one of rejuvenation and restored youth. The Immortal Ark elders were already accustomed to it, but Li Fan narrowed his eyes thoughtfully.

Jian Daosheng did not remain until he had completely reverted to his youthful years. After staying within the influence of the [Ancient] character for only a few breaths, he gracefully retreated. Li Fan understood the reason. It was to prevent himself from being influenced too deeply by the character's power.

After Jian Daosheng, the other Immortal Ark elders also took turns approaching the original inscription. Some stayed longer, some shorter, but all became noticeably younger."With this character, wouldn't one be able to remain forever young and immortal?" Li Fan exclaimed.

Zhong Daogong smiled bitterly."It is not as simple as it appears. Remaining youthful is possible, but immortality..."He shook his head."Using the [Ancient] character for rejuvenation can indeed increase one's lifespan limit somewhat. However, the more times it is used, the weaker the effect becomes.

Once one's lifespan reaches its ultimate limit, it can no longer be extended.""Even so, possessing a youthful body is vastly preferable to existing on the verge of death."After all the Immortal Ark elders had finished, Zhong Daogong looked toward Li Fan and asked:"Would Fellow Daoist like to experience it as well?"Li Fan declined politely."I am only thirty years old.

This is already the peak period of my body. Returning to childhood would be harmful instead."The moment those words were spoken, complete silence fell over the Divine Tree space. Every elder stared at Li Fan as though looking at a monster. Even Zhong Daogong's expression stiffened slightly."Fellow Daoist must be joking, right?"Based on Li Fan's strength, along with the attainments he had displayed in formations and seal script characters, the people of the Immortal Ark had long assumed he was a cultivator who had lived for several hundred or even several thousand years.

Now Li Fan was claiming he was only thirty years old. How could they possibly accept that? Li Fan chuckled."I merely learn things a bit faster. Although there are not many, there are still people in Great Qi who are even more talented than I am. There is no need for everyone to be so surprised."His words left the Immortal Ark cultivators thoroughly shaken.

After jointly resealing the [Ancient] character, they escorted Li Fan out from within the Divine Tree."Fellow Daoist, please rest here for now and refrain from leaving temporarily. We still have some matters that require discussion..."At the highest level of the Immortal Ark space, within a pavilion courtyard resembling one atop a mountain peak, Zhong Daogong gave these instructions."Oh, right.

These are the three items you selected earlier."After handing over a scroll of white paper, Zhong Daogong hurried away. Ignoring the Immortal Ark cultivators, Li Fan slowly unfolded the white paper in his hand. The three treasures immediately leaped out from the scroll and floated before him.

He had already carefully examined the bronze immortal crane and the seven-colored stream of light. With a wave of his hand, he directly stored them away. His attention then focused on the figurine with downcast eyes. Sensing the faint fluctuations transmitted by [Truth], Li Fan's mind became focused.

Just like the previous two items, the figurine vanished in an instant. This time, however, it was not stored away. Instead, it transformed into a mysterious energy and was absorbed by [Truth].Li Fan stared closely at the [Truth] interface. Tiny currents seemed to flash across it as the characters on the panel flickered several times.

Then they settled once more. Li Fan's gaze fell upon the section that had changed."Virtualization Charging Progress: 400%.""Hm?"Li Fan's eyes narrowed. Virtualization Charging Progress was the prerequisite for activating [Truth] and reversing everything.

Only when the charge reached 100% could illusion become reality and reality become illusion. This was also why, after every [Truth], Li Fan would remain hidden within the Great Xuan Small World until the charging process was complete before venturing outside.

Previously, the maximum charge limit had been 200%.Any charge beyond 100% could, at critical moments, shield Li Fan from external attacks and ensure the successful activation of [Truth].However, as the enemies Li Fan provoked grew increasingly powerful, this protective function had become rather insignificant.

The charge depleted too quickly and provided only marginal benefit. Yet now, this figurine had directly doubled the maximum charge limit. Furthermore, Li Fan could sense that if this additional 200% charge remained unused during this life, it would not disappear when [Truth] reset everything.

Instead, it would remain within [Truth] itself. In other words, he could use it to perform three consecutive [Truth] reversions."Interesting."Li Fan recalled the appearance of that puppet.'The origin of this puppet is rather strange. Something capable of increasing the charging limit by 200 percent is definitely no ordinary object.''I just wonder, if I encounter it again in the next life and absorb it once more, will the charging limit continue to increase?'Up to this point, Li Fan still had not figured out the exact mechanism behind the charging of [Truth].The unknown medium required to activate [Truth] could only be accumulated slowly through the passage of time.'Perhaps by carefully studying this puppet, I can uncover the mystery.'Li Fan narrowed his eyes and mentally recorded this objective.

A 400 percent charging limit was no different from having an extra layer of protection. More importantly, when facing beings such as the Faceless True Immortal or even higher existences, Li Fan could use [Truth] several times in succession, maximizing his chances of escaping their threat.

Otherwise, if he possessed only a single use of [Truth], then after returning to the designated anchor point, he would still have to face the same deadly danger. In that case, his situation would become extremely perilous. From this perspective, the life-saving value of this puppet could not be overstated.'The Immortal Ark truly is my blessed land.''Later, I can also ask them about the origin of this puppet.'Li Fan thought to himself.

Not long afterward, Zhong Daogong led a group of elders back before Li Fan. It seemed that their previous discussion had reached some kind of consensus.
