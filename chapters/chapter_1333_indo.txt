No matter how unbelievable it seemed, the Dharma-Transmitters ultimately had no choice but to accept this fact. Out of reverence for the Immortal Ancestor, however, none of them dared discuss it openly. They were even less willing to take the initiative to send a message asking the Immortal Ancestor about it.

In the end, they could only conclude that the Immortal Ancestor cared deeply for all living beings of the Xuanhuang Realm. Even the slightest disturbance was enough for him to issue a warning. After all, some unrest had indeed occurred. What the countless living beings of the Xuanhuang Realm could not perceive was that, beyond the void, an immense quantity of True Immortal power had quietly accumulated.

Li Fan stood before the now completely filled depression. Feeling the faint attractive force emanating from it, he nodded in satisfaction. This harvest had exceeded even his own expectations. The naturally formed depression had become an almost perfect vessel for storing True Immortal power.

Coupled with the formations Li Fan had arranged around its perimeter to conceal it, every abnormality in this place was perfectly hidden. However, Li Fan could use the Ascension Formation as an intermediary to transform this enormous power into the driving force that would elevate the Xuanhuang Realm. In this way, the consumption of True Immortal flesh and blood would be greatly reduced.

The entire plan might fail at the final moment.""I also need to find a way to increase the production of True Immortal flesh and blood and source power essence simultaneously."Using a mortal body to accomplish the work of a True Immortal naturally came with countless limitations.

Although Li Fan's accumulation over many reincarnations allowed him to find all kinds of extraordinary substitute powers, they were ultimately not true Immortal Spirit Power. As he drew closer and closer to his objective, everything became increasingly troublesome.

As for how to temporarily anchor the world at a higher level, Li Fan still had no clues. Putting aside the Enlightened True Immortal within the Immortal Ruins, from the perspective of higher dimensions, the highest points throughout the entire Darkest Star Sea were likely only the Remnant World Great Wall beneath the High Wall, along with the place deep within the Dark Sea of the Xuanhuang Realm where everything converged.

Naturally, there were also some naturally formed mountain peaks scattered throughout the Star Sea. Although he had already absorbed most of her memories and undoubtedly understood her better than before, this female Dharma King still gave Li Fan a greater sense of danger than Mo Rubin.

By comparison, Mo Rubin, with whom Li Fan had dealt across several reincarnations, was much easier to control. With a flick of his finger, the phantom of the Wooden Sword left its sheath. Li Fan brought Mo Rubin with him into the void beyond the Xuanhuang Realm.

He then slowly withdrew the golden net that had imprisoned him. Exposed to the cold, silent Darkest Star Sea, Mo Rubin instinctively sensed danger and slowly awakened. Almost the moment he regained consciousness, he noticed Li Fan standing not far away."Hm?

A mirror-like boundary hidden within the darkness silently came into existence and locked onto Li Fan. This was one of the divine abilities contained within the Innumerable Calamities Undying Demon Heart Immortal Art. It could replace the person reflected on the opposite side of the mirror and transform them into another Demon Heart belonging to its user.

As long as someone was enveloped by it, regardless of cultivation level, none could escape. This confidence came from the Immortal Realm inheritance Mo Rubin had received. However, he quickly realized something was wrong, and his expression changed slightly.

The mirror had indeed enveloped Li Fan. Yet on the other side of the mirror, Li Fan's reflection did not exist at all. It was as though the person before him was merely an illusion that did not exist within the real world. Yet paradoxically, the vitality radiating openly from Li Fan clearly proved that he was real.

The Transformation of Truth and Falsehood surpassed even the [Creation] True Immortal seal script. Naturally, it was far beyond anything Mo Rubin could comprehend. At this moment, the Xuantian Sect Dharma King only knew that he had encountered an extraordinarily troublesome opponent.

After several more attempts still failed to lock onto Li Fan, he decisively gave up. Without making the slightest sound, he withdrew the mirror boundary divine ability."Fellow Daoist Mo, there is no need to be so nervous.
