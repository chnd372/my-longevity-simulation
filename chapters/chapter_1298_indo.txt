Bab 1297: The Holy Legacy Shares a Common Hatred

The cultivators who raised their heads to look at the sky all discovered, without exception, that the heavens seemed close enough to touch. Moreover, the concentration of spiritual energy throughout the world had just undergone an extremely obvious decline.

The Xuanhuang winds and rains had also weakened significantly, as part of the world's power had been drawn away. The overwhelming majority of cultivators in the world had no idea why such a terrifying phenomenon had occurred. Only the few standing at the peak of the Xuanhuang Realm's power understood what these phenomena truly represented.

There, a glowing cloud mass representing the current Xuanhuang floated silently. The many changes that had occurred in the Xuanhuang Realm since the beginning of the war were all displayed in meticulous detail within the cloud mass.

The Xuanhuang Realm, a single world. Though it had stitched together and absorbed many other surviving cultivation worlds, its scale ultimately had limits.

Every wound was exactly the same as the black circular scar on his palm from before.
