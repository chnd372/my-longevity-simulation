A monstrous aura of evil surged from it. Its face possessed no actual features. Only countless pale arms extended outward, constantly waving. Every time Sun Erlang's group killed a demonic cultivator, another arm would emerge from the statue's face. It was as if the dead demonic cultivators were actually being devoured by the giant statue.
