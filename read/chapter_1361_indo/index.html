Bab 1360: The Mortal Cauldron Transforms into an Immortal Go Piece

The anger and unwillingness were gone. Whether Dao Integration, Soul Transformation, or Nascent Soul cultivators. Under the Immortal Descends, All Things Return to Void Grand Formation, every cultivator of the Medicine King Sect displayed the same reaction.

Fear. Only endless fear filled their hearts. Only after Li Fan withdrew the overwhelming might of the world destroying formation did the members of the Medicine King Sect gradually recover from that extreme state of mind. Yet with Li Fan remaining silent, none of them dared move in the slightest.

The power of the immortal formation continued to circulate. Though its destructive force had been withdrawn, the formation itself remained. It created an independent space that enveloped the entire Medicine King Sect. While suppressing everyone present, it also isolated the rules of the Xuanhuang Realm that prevented different cultivation paths from being cultivated simultaneously, preventing the newly awakened Medicine King Sect cultivators from falling into mutual slaughter.

Li Fan then ignored them completely and focused on the tiniest particles of the Medicine King True Cauldron. Just as he had expected, they eventually gathered together and formed the shape of a single Go piece. However, unlike the Go pieces Li Fan had previously collected, this one was not covered in a layer of gray.

Instead, it was unmistakably...White. Along the edge of its white surface was even a ring of colors that closely resembled the fused Go piece. Li Fan reached out and drew the white Go piece into his hand. Gazing at it carefully, he sensed boundless vitality contained within its tiny form.

The Go pieces he had previously subdued within his body, including the already fused Immortal Realm fragment, all began to tremble gently. It was as if they had finally welcomed their true core, erupting in waves of joyful celebration. An even stronger urge than before radiated from the Go pieces inside him.

They could hardly wait to merge with this white Go piece. That urging gradually transformed into Li Fan's own desire. The longing became so intense that even continuously operating the Immortal Heart Mantra could barely suppress it. But Li Fan's willpower was extraordinary.

With immense determination, he forcibly suppressed the restless movement of the Go pieces within his body. Calming his mind, he carefully examined the white Go piece in his hand and compared it with the six Go pieces sealed within his body. Inside Li Fan's body were one large Go piece and five smaller ones.

The large one shone with seven colors, formed from the fusion of six ordinary gray Go pieces. The five smaller Go pieces revolved around the colorful one. The white Go piece before him, although only a single piece, gave Li Fan the same feeling as that fused seven colored Go piece."It seems the piece Sun Piaomiao left behind in the Xuanhuang Realm was the most crucial one.""A complete Immortal Realm...""The only remaining piece is the one inside the Xuan Immortal Ark."At the thought that, years from now, he would once again witness a fragment of the long destroyed Immortal Realm, even Li Fan could not suppress the excitement rising in his heart."Does this fused Immortal Realm contain immortal spiritual qi?""Can it be used to ascend and become an immortal?""Are there relics left behind by the True Immortal who forged these Go pieces?""Or perhaps...

is there even a True Immortal inside, waiting to be reborn together with this Immortal Realm?"Countless questions flooded Li Fan's mind like a tide. An ordinary cultivator, even after obtaining an Immortal Realm fragment, would undoubtedly hesitate in the face of its unknown dangers and mysteries, never daring to activate it lightly.

But Li Fan, who possessed [Truth], had no such concerns. Especially in this lifetime, now that his immortal formation had finally been completed, [Truth]'s charging efficiency and protective power had doubled, giving him ample means of protection....The white Go piece transformed from the Medicine King Cauldron possessed an irresistible attraction, drawing the gaze of every cultivator present.

They could vaguely sense that an enormous secret lay behind it. Yet after witnessing Li Fan's overwhelming display of power, none of them dared approach. They could only watch from afar. In the end, it was Liu Ruchen, who had finally recovered somewhat, who gathered his courage and spoke."Senior, may I ask, is this Go piece a relic left behind by Ancestor Sun Piaomiao?"The reason Liu Ruchen dared speak was because, before destroying the Medicine King Cauldron, Li Fan had deliberately released all the Medicine King Sect cultivators imprisoned within it.

He had shown sufficient goodwill. Li Fan sealed the white Go piece separately from the others within his body before letting out a cold laugh."Sun Piaomiao entrusted this object to you descendants for so many years. He even left countless hints both openly and secretly.

Yet none of you ever discovered its true nature.""You truly are a group of useless fools."Li Fan's merciless rebuke also indirectly answered Liu Ruchen's question. Most of the Medicine King Sect disciples lowered their heads in shame. Liu Ruchen smiled awkwardly and was about to ask more when Li Fan cut him off."This matter is no longer something you are qualified to participate in."Li Fan narrowed his eyes as he looked at Liu Ruchen."Although you violated the founding principles of the Medicine King Sect, by leading the entire sect away from the Xuanhuang Realm, you accidentally accomplished a great deed."With a flick of his finger, countless scenes instantly appeared before everyone.

They depicted the tremendous changes that had occurred throughout the Xuanhuang Realm and the Star Sea during the ten thousand years that the Medicine King Cauldron had been trapped within the Immortal Ruins. The entire Medicine King Sect immediately erupted into an uproar.

When Li Fan slightly relaxed the immortal formation's suppression, they immediately felt overwhelming killing intent erupt from within themselves toward their relatives, friends, masters, and fellow disciples. Only then did they realize that Li Fan had spoken the truth.

Their faces turned deathly pale as despair overtook them. Li Fan ignored their reactions and instead summoned the seven Dao Integration cultivators of the Medicine King Sect, including Liu Ruchen."Do you know what this Go piece formed from the Medicine King Cauldron truly is?""It is one of the fragments of the former Immortal Realm left behind by Sun Piaomiao."His opening statement alone left the Dao Integration cultivators completely stunned.

Li Fan then slightly released a trace of the aura from the seven colored Go piece sealed within his body. Even that tiny fragment of Immortal Realm laws was enough to leave the Medicine King Sect Dao Integration cultivators utterly intoxicated."The Immortal Realm's great calamity showed signs long ago.

Back then, Sun Piaomiao received the mandate of the immortals and left behind preparations for the revival of the Immortal Realm."Li Fan calmly recounted what he claimed to be the truth, though it was merely his own speculation."Now that Sun Piaomiao has disappeared, you, as his disciples, naturally have the duty to assist me in restoring the Immortal Realm."Li Fan looked down upon them as though it were the most natural thing in the world.

Liu Ruchen and the others had only just been immersed in the mysteries of Immortal Realm laws. Now, hearing Li Fan's words, they were completely dumbfounded. They all stared at him with disbelief."Restore the Immortal Realm? Us?""I have already gathered all the fragments of the Immortal Realm.""We are merely waiting for the proper opportunity.""When the time comes, you need only assist from the side."Li Fan spoke calmly.

Faced with the request of someone who was seemingly a True Immortal, how could the Dao Integration cultivators of the Medicine King Sect dare refuse? Still, they were filled with doubt. Such a terrifying expert could destroy their entire sect with a casual wave of his hand.

Why would he need their assistance? Yet since Li Fan did not explain, they did not dare ask. Were these members of the Medicine King Sect useful? To Li Fan, they certainly were. However, not for rebuilding the Immortal Realm. Instead, they would serve as catalysts for merging the various powers together when the time came to elevate the Xuanhuang Realm.
