It transformed into a throne of starlight. Yin Shangren scratched his scalp. Hiss..."The Holy Emperor's transmission of the Dao lasted for three days and three nights. The enlightened people listened as though intoxicated and entranced. Even after the Holy Emperor's image dispersed, they still remained immersed within it, completely unaware.

For the vast majority of them, the Holy Emperor's teachings would require the remainder of their lives to fully digest. At this moment, the Holy Emperor had already returned to the Holy Emperor Throne. His six disciples stood in order below him."Congratulations to Master on attaining the Dao!"
