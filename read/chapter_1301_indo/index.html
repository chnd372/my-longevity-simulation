Bab 1300: Golden Needle Reverses Xuanhuang

"The Watcher bears the mission of the Immortal Realm, and is very likely even a creation directly made by a True Immortal. It is not something mortal power can contend against.""Although the Immortal Path has long since been severed, as long as they still exist, they will forever resolutely carry out their mission.

This is precisely our hope for escaping this predicament..." Zhong Daogong rapidly wrote in the air."This Xuanhuang Realm Watcher does not seem to actively attack us. As long as we make no sound and do not attract its attention, we should be able to remain safe for the time being.

However, staying in this cauldron forever is not a long term solution either. I can sense that the longer we remain here, the faster our life force is slowly draining away.""The Watcher only cares about its mission. We must quickly determine what exactly the mission of the thing above us is.

Only then can we think of a way to escape..."Everyone raised their heads, their gazes passing through the Xuan Immortal Ark and landing on the terrifying figure at the edge of the giant cauldron, stirring the water within."Do not observe it, lest unforeseen changes occur." Elder Gan Jingda waved his hand, temporarily blocking everyone's vision.

Nangong Lie of the Dao Attainment Academy wrote with a solemn expression, "If we use the power of True Immortal seal script characters to create chaos, is there any hope of escaping? No matter how strong the Watcher is, it should not be able to resist True Immortal power, right?"Zhong Daogong shook his head slightly and explained, "Indeed, it should not be able to completely resist the influence of True Immortal seal script.

The [Deception] character I used just now already proved this point. But after all, it has countless connections with the Immortal Realm. Compared to us mortal cultivators, the Watcher does possess a certain resistance to True Immortal power.""The Watcher before us, after being struck by the [Deception] character, merely forgot what had happened before, yet did not leave this cauldron..." Zhong Daogong's meaning was self evident.

Ai Tianjue also nodded in agreement. "Correct. Using True Immortal seal script to create chaos is far too risky. Just one glance from it already wiped out one tenth of our ark's population. If it can resist even for a moment... even if we manage to escape, not many aboard the Ark will survive."Zhong Daogong continued, "Everyone, you must also consider what happens after we escape from here.

If we exhaust all the accumulated True Immortal seal script characters, how will we contend against the Dharma-Transmitting Immortal Ancestor of the Xuanhuang Realm?""Yes, this grudge must definitely be repaid. Even if we cannot obtain immortal spiritual qi, we absolutely must seize some resources from the Xuanhuang Realm." Workshop Master Zhang of the Immortal Ark's Forging Star Workshop wore a vicious expression....After deliberation among the Ark's elders, a consensus was finally reached.

Personnel would first be sent out of the cauldron to investigate the surrounding environment. The goal was to determine the possible mission of the terrifying Watcher before them. At the same time, they would remain prepared at all times for the Watcher to discover the Ark, forcing a desperate final battle.

Very quickly, twenty cultivators left the Ark one after another, flying toward the outside of the cauldron. Some of them did not even make it beyond the range of the giant cauldron before being drawn by invisible vortices in the water and falling in, disappearing completely.

As for those strong enough to fly beyond the cauldron, the vast majority were discovered by the invisible Watcher, had their life force directly extracted, and were squeezed into little balls. Only one cultivator, who luckily escaped the Watcher's range, never returned.

The Xuan Immortal Ark then sent out another hundred cultivators. Although three of them successfully completed the reconnaissance this time, on their return journey, the moment they tried to reenter the cauldron, they were all discovered by the Watcher without exception.

They were squeezed into spheres and died instantly."This kind of meaningless sacrifice is not a solution.""We should just fight it outright.""Those too weak cannot even fly out of the cauldron. Those too strong will definitely be noticed by the Watcher.""Especially Dao Entrant elders.

Going means certain death."...The atmosphere aboard the Ark once again sank into despair. At this moment, Zhao Xian, the body possessed by Holy Embryo Li Fan, stepped forward. Li Fan looked at Ai Tianjue and resolutely wrote, "Teacher, I want to try."The elders of the Severed Immortal Tower all showed moved expressions.

They had an extremely favorable impression of this exceptionally talented young junior. Ai Tianjue raised his hand and rejected Li Fan's request. The Severed Immortal Tower currently held the highest authority aboard the Ark, and Li Fan was a direct descendant of their faction.

Naturally, he received some preferential treatment. Zhong Daogong overruled the objections and sent out another reconnaissance group of three hundred. The result was still total annihilation. Watching one living cultivator after another mercilessly squeezed into spheres by the Watcher, the cold terror in everyone's hearts had already accumulated to a certain threshold.

When Zhong Daogong prepared to issue another order, seeing the trembling bodies and despair in the cultivators' eyes, he hesitated. It was not because he had grown soft hearted, but because even he had begun to doubt whether such probing had any meaning at all.

At this moment, Li Fan stepped forward once more."Elder Zhong, this student is willing to try." Though a trace of fear initially flashed in Li Fan's eyes, it was quickly replaced by incomparable determination. The elders of the Severed Immortal Tower looked at the cultivators of the other Ark organizations and inwardly sighed.

In the end, they agreed to Li Fan's request. After a brief farewell to his teachers and seniors, under the watchful gaze of everyone, Li Fan calmly flew out of the Xuan Immortal Ark."Brother Tianjue, you accepted a fine disciple."Elder Zhong, who rarely initiated conversation with others, spoke to Ai Tianjue this time.

Ai Tianjue nodded slightly, his expression complex. Watching Li Fan successfully leave the giant cauldron, he let out a breath of relief. What followed was a long wait. As time passed, everyone still did not see Li Fan's returning figure. The flame of hope slowly dimmed.

Just as Zhong Daogong assumed Li Fan had also failed and died, and was preparing to send out one final reconnaissance group, a figure openly flew past the Watcher stirring the cauldron's waters. Descending from the sky, he returned to the Xuan Immortal Ark.

It was Li Fan. Although the Watcher was overhead and no one dared make noise, the expressions on everyone's faces fully revealed the excitement in their hearts. After losing nearly five hundred of the Ark's backbone cultivators, they had finally seen hope of escape.

Like stars surrounding the moon, they welcomed Li Fan to the center of the elders. Li Fan did not rest, and immediately used his finger as a pen to rapidly recount what he had seen and experienced outside."Nine Yin Yang Pearls?""Each one seems to contain endless frozen corpses?"...The scenes Li Fan described filled the Ark cultivators with horror.

However, several elders from the Celestial Pivot Institute frowned, seemingly recalling something. After inviting Zhong Daogong over for a discussion. Zhong Daogong gathered everyone and declared, "Everyone, perhaps we have found a way to escape this place.""This Watcher's mission...""Should be collecting different specimens."Zhong Daogong's expression did not resemble the joy of having found a path to escape.

Instead, it was extremely grim."What do you mean by different specimens, Elder Zhong?" The crowd all revealed puzzled expressions. Zhong Gongdao said, "Dean Jian, you should explain the situation."Everyone's attention immediately focused on the white-haired Dean of the Celestial Pivot Institute, Jian Daosheng.

Jian Daosheng's eyes showed a trace of reminiscence as he wrote out his explanation."During the great ancient age of cultivation, the Star Sea flourished, and countless cultivation worlds brimmed with life. Yet our Xuanshang Immortal Ark always willingly drifted through the Star Sea, without any fixed home.""The world believed that Xuanshang people pursued profit, and that was why we traveled between worlds to seek enormous gains.

But...""Would it not be far more beneficial for commerce to have a fixed world as a base?""The lives of everyone aboard the Xuanshang Immortal Ark were tied to a single immortal ark. Although the Star Sea of that era was not as perilous as it is now, drifting through the Star Sea year after year inevitably brought calamities.""Yet we still preferred wandering across the Star Sea rather than settling in a single world.""That was simply because the Xuanshang Immortal Ark was founded by a group of cultivators who had escaped from a cultivation world where Watchers existed."Jian Daosheng sighed as he looked at the shocked expressions of everyone present, then continued writing."Cultivators treat mortals like cattle and horses, raising them in captivity.

As for the Immortal Realm's treatment of cultivators, it may be even worse.""Nearly every somewhat prosperous cultivation world bears traces of surveillance from the Immortal Realm. Even those remote and nameless cultivation worlds, no one can guarantee that immortals will not suddenly descend and interfere.

What exactly the Xuanshang ancestors discovered before fleeing their original homeland is no longer known. But one thing is certain. It was out of fear of the Immortal Realm, and unwillingness to be raised like livestock, that the Xuanshang ancestors chose to live aboard the Immortal Ark.""At the height of its prosperity, the Immortal Ark only had a population of one million.

Perhaps because the number was simply too small, for a very long time there truly was no intervention from the Immortal Realm."Jian Daosheng shifted the topic toward the reason for this speculation."Judging from what Zhao Xian saw, those frozen within the nine Yin-Yang Pearls should be cultivators who have existed in the Xuanhuang Realm from ancient times until now.""Not dead, but have existed." Jian Daosheng deliberately emphasized this point."That is why the disciples who previously attempted to escape by concealing their vitality failed.""If we want to escape from the sight of this Xuanhuang Watcher, the safest method is to disguise ourselves as cultivators who already exist within those nine frozen Yin-Yang Pearls."...The expressions of those aboard the Immortal Ark varied.

After all, this was the force that had governed the Immortal Ark for thousands of years before the Severed Immortal Tower. Out of trust in the Celestial Pivot Institute, everyone decided to try this method. Fortunately, when Li Fan had investigated earlier, his divine sense had swept across the endless frozen ocean and recorded enough cultivator characteristics.

After the disguises were successfully prepared, the first group of cultivators set out. Unlike Li Fan, who had strode about openly, they moved with extreme caution, advancing step by step. And that invisible Watcher truly seemed to completely ignore them.

The entire group of thirty successfully escaped that nightmare-like giant cauldron. With the method proven viable, everyone aboard the Xuan Immortal Ark accelerated their escape. Once all members of the Immortal Ark had escaped, the Watcher's act of stirring the water within the giant cauldron finally came to an end.

It seemed to extend a hand and violently strike the cauldron's body. Amid enormous reverberations, the giant cauldron slowly began to shrink. The Watcher struck it repeatedly, and the giant cauldron became smaller and smaller. In the end, it turned into a small transparent pearl.

Inside the pearl, the former cauldron water became a calm ocean. After completing all this, the watcher first stood there in a daze for a while. Only after a long time did it seem to remember its mission. With thunderous footsteps, it left this space. The people of the Xuan Immortal Ark, who had held their breath while watching everything, only finally let out a long sigh of relief after confirming the watcher had truly departed.

But to avoid attracting the Watcher back again, everyone still communicated through gestures."Elder Zhong, we have already investigated. This is an enclosed space, and there are no exits around us.""We still have not figured out how that Watcher left."...With the united efforts of everyone aboard the Xuan Immortal Ark, whose inheritance had endured for thousands of years, it did not take long to determine that the exit lay within the nine Yin-Yang Pearls.

After carefully selecting one of them, the Xuan Immortal Ark flew inside. When they personally saw the endless corpses across the frozen ocean, everyone aboard the Immortal Ark could not suppress the fear in their hearts. Inside the Yin-Yang Pearl, finally away from the Watcher once again, they could at last communicate through divine sense."Why is the Xuanhuang Watcher collecting so many corpses?""This seems to be some profound formation.""It seems the Xuanshang ancestors made the right choice back then.

In the eyes of True Immortals, cultivators are no different from livestock."...As the people aboard the Immortal Ark exchanged thoughts, Li Fan sent a divine sense transmission to Ai Tianjue."Teacher, are there really no records left of what the Xuanshang ancestors discovered back then?"Li Fan had keenly noticed that Dean Jian and the several elders of the Severed Immortal Tower seemed to have exchanged something earlier.

That was why he asked. A trace of hesitation flashed in Ai Tianjue's eyes. Ordinarily, Li Fan was not qualified to know this secret of the immortal ark. But Li Fan had risked his life to scout the path for the Immortal Ark and had successfully brought back crucial information.

Calling him the hero who saved the Xuan Immortal Ark would not be excessive. So there was no need to hide it. After discussing with the other elders and finally obtaining permission, Ai Tianjue replied."Dean Jian was not lying. The ancestral instructions truly are no longer aboard the Xuan Immortal Ark..."But Li Fan caught the meaning in Ai Tianjue's words."No longer aboard the Xuan Immortal Ark?

Teacher, you mean...""They are aboard the Shang Immortal Ark?"Ai Tianjue nodded."Back then, during the civil conflict aboard the immortal ark, it split into two. Although our Xuan Immortal Ark seized the Hidden Immortal Compendium of the Hundred Tales, the most important things from the Xuanshang Immortal Ark fell into the hands of the Shang Immortal Ark."Even someone with Ai Tianjue's temperament felt slight regret when speaking of this matter."The most important things?" Li Fan's eyes flickered.

Ai Tianjue's gaze showed a trace of yearning."After the Xuanshang ancestors fled their homeland, they feared being tracked by the Watchers, or even by True Immortals. So while drifting endlessly through the Star Sea, they also sought methods to completely escape the True Immortals.""As the saying goes, immortals and mortals are fundamentally different.

Even Dao Integration cultivators are no different from ants before True Immortals. Wanting to escape True Immortals through ordinary means is nothing but a fool's dream.""But the Xuanshang ancestors truly succeeded."A glimmer of understanding flashed through Li Fan's eyes."Immortal artifacts?""Correct.

And more than just that." Ai Tianjue sighed."You also know that in ancient times, some cultivation worlds had especially close ties with the upper realm. As time passed, some immortal artifacts became scattered everywhere. The Xuanshang Immortal Ark traversed the Star Sea collecting immortal artifacts, and attempted to analyze their power and make it their own.""They definitely achieved some success back then.

Unfortunately, before they could turn that research into practical application, upheaval struck the Star Sea.""The Xuanshang Immortal Ark descended into civil conflict and split in two. Our Xuan Immortal Ark seized the vast majority of stored supplies, namely the Hidden Immortal Compendium of the Hundred Tales.

The Shang Immortal Ark took possession of the research insights from that era, along with an unknown number of immortal artifacts."Li Fan was slightly stunned."So that means in the civil conflict back then, our Xuan Immortal Ark were actually the losers?"Ai Tianjue nodded."You could say that."Li Fan then fell silent.

He thought to himself, "No wonder everyone aboard the Xuan Immortal Ark has always been extremely secretive about information regarding the Shang Immortal Ark."After a long while, Li Fan revealed a puzzled expression and asked again, "The Xuanshang Immortal Ark drifted in the star sea for so long.

How could it fall into internal conflict merely because a calamity descended?""Even when our Xuan Immortal Ark previously faced the Watchers in what was nearly a hopeless situation, internal chaos never broke out. The Xuanshang Immortal Ark of that time should not have been inferior to us, right?""That is because the navigator of the Xuanshang Immortal Ark intended to do a completely insane thing that would drag everyone aboard to their deaths," Ai Tianjue said coldly."Hm?""They intended to steer the Xuanshang Immortal Ark directly into the High Wall, attempting to break out of this star sea." After a moment of silence, Ai Tianjue spoke slowly.

Li Fan could not help but be stunned....Just as everyone aboard the Xuan Immortal Ark was still searching for a way out within the Yin-Yang Pearl. The changes in the Xuanhuang Realm had already entered a new stage. The Holy Emperor, who had been fighting the Dharma-Transmitting Immortal Ancestor to a temporary stalemate, was now forced to split his attention and look toward the strange phenomenon in the sky.

The fusion of the many remnant worlds had already been preliminarily completed. The white mist barrier that had originally stretched across heaven and earth shattered one after another under the crushing force of the Xuanhuang Realm's internal power of growth and decline.

The boundless sky encompassing all prefectures was revealed before the cultivators of Xuanhuang for the first time. The immortal spiritual qi scattered throughout the world had already been completely absorbed by the Xuanhuang heaven and earth. The cultivators also gradually awakened from their greed and madness.

They stood frozen in place, blankly staring at the pristine sky they had never seen before. Rumble...Amid the slight trembling of heaven and earth, it seemed as though something had been activated. Lines flashed across the heavenly dome one after another.

Even with the Holy Emperor's strength, he was unable to discern whether they were real or illusory. The Immortal Ancestor's attacks became increasingly fierce. Li Ping could sense that Immortal Ancestor was gradually no longer holding back. Understanding in his heart that the most critical moment was approaching, the Holy Emperor exhausted all his strength in an attempt to obstruct the Immortal Ancestor.

The roaring of heaven and earth grew louder and louder. Yet within the Holy Emperor's heart, a sudden sense of unease arose. Looking at this sky, Li Ping unexpectedly developed the feeling that he was inside a formation. Just as the entire Great Qi world existed beneath the Grand Formation of Heaven and Earth Myriad Spirits.

And at the very moment the Holy Emperor had this thought, an extremely subtle fluctuation of consciousness instantly appeared in his mind. It came from the Xuanhuang Realm itself. Although it vanished in an instant, Li Ping was able to discern the meaning the Xuanhuang Heavenly Dao wished to convey.

Danger. Escape. Li Ping could not understand. The Xuanhuang Realm was currently stronger than ever before. So where could the danger possibly come from? While Li Ping was momentarily distracted, a streak of golden light suddenly arrived from beyond the heavens.

Like a golden needle, it flew at extreme speed and viciously pierced into the Xuanhuang earth. The roaring of the Xuanhuang Realm came to an abrupt halt. It was as though this single needle had forcibly interrupted the Xuanhuang Realm's transformation process.

A surge of anger immediately rose within the Holy Emperor's heart. Starlight surged around his body as he instantly flashed past Immortal Ancestor. He arrived before that golden needle. Source power essence enveloped his palm as he attempted to pull the golden light out of the Xuanhuang earth.

Yet what left the Holy Emperor utterly astonished was that although this was clearly a golden needle from beyond the heavens, it seemed like one of the Xuanhuang Realm's own meridians and blood vessels. It had fused into one with the Xuanhuang Realm. As he applied slight force, the Xuanhuang earth beneath his feet began to shake violently.

Mountains rose and fell, and the power of the earth veins burst forth. Seeing this, the Holy Emperor loosened his grip and abandoned the attempt to forcefully pull it out. And while he was attempting to remove the needle, countless golden needles descended from beyond the heavens like a meteor shower.

One after another, arranged according to some profoundly mysterious pattern, they pierced into the Xuanhuang earth. Not only was the Xuanhuang Realm's transformation process interrupted. Whirlpools appeared in the sky, and the immortal spiritual qi that had already been absorbed by the Xuanhuang heaven and earth was actually being forced back out.

At the locations where the golden needles stood, strands of white mist began to emerge once more. Like sharp surgical blades, they began cutting apart the Xuanhuang earth again. A strange force was slowly enveloping the Xuanhuang Realm. After witnessing the immortal spiritual qi being released once more, the living beings within the Xuanhuang Realm did not scramble to seize it.

Instead, their minds became dazed and unfocused. Many living beings directly collapsed to the ground and fell into deep sleep. Unable to personally witness this profoundly mysterious transformation of heaven and earth."Heavenly Doctor!" The Holy Emperor had already understood the mastermind behind this event.

He raised his head toward the sky, attempting to locate the other party. However...The Immortal Ancestor's attack arrived in an instant. This time, there was no longer any restraint. The maximum force that the Xuanhuang Realm could barely endure erupted explosively."Heavenly Sovereign has arrived, so why do you not kneel!"Immortal Ancestor's incomparably cold voice rang out simultaneously.

As though it emerged from every corner of heaven and earth at once. The Holy Emperor's body instantly stiffened. It was as though he were facing the direct gaze and questioning of every cultivator in heaven and earth who practiced the new cultivation path.

Those countless eyes of the masses seemed like chains, tightly wrapping around his body."So the Xuanhuang Realm is the true home ground of the Dharma-Transmitting Heavenly Sovereign..." A flash of understanding passed through the Holy Emperor's mind."However, within this Xuanhuang Realm, I also have assistance."The Holy Emperor's robe of starlight surged.

He roared, "Mo Rubin, how much longer do you intend to keep watching the show!"Although he did not know where exactly this Dharma King of the Xuantian Sect was hiding. But Li Ping was certain that when the upheaval in the Xuanhuang Realm occurred, he would definitely be hiding in some corner, silently watching everything.

From his brief earlier exchange with Mo Rubin, Li Ping could tell that this Xuantian Sect Dharma King did not particularly care about any so-called Xuanhuang orthodoxy. As long as the world beneath his feet remained intact, that was enough. What he cared about more was finding that vanished Xuantian King and leaving this Darkest Star Sea.

So the Holy Emperor paid no attention to whether Mo Rubin responded or not, and inwardly shouted, "Dao One!"The spiritual energy between heaven and earth instantly came alive. Released through the Five Elements Great Cave Heaven, and with the Dao One insects that had already spread throughout the entire world during the Xuanhuang Realm's previous savage growth, they now answered the Holy Emperor's summons.

From death to life, they surged in a great torrent toward the Holy Emperor's body. Like an abyss swallowing the ocean, the Holy Emperor's body gradually expanded as the insect-transformed spiritual energy continuously poured into him. Ten zhang, one hundred zhang, one thousand zhang...The Immortal Ancestor chains wrapped around his body instantly turned to ash.

In the blink of an eye, the Holy Emperor had become a truly colossal giant standing between heaven and earth. Overlooking the lands of Xuanhuang. Yet the infusion of insect-transformed spiritual energy still did not stop. It was as if he truly intended to devour all the spiritual energy of the Xuanhuang Realm within a short span of time.

As his body continued to expand, the vast merged Xuanhuang Realm gradually appeared no larger than a single room in the Holy Emperor's eyes. Even the attacks of the Immortal Ancestor no longer seemed so impossible to resist. The Holy Emperor now had enough spare energy to look beyond Xuanhuang and search for the Heavenly Doctor's location."I will hold him back!"At this moment, Tianyang's voice suddenly rang out.

He stepped in front of the Immortal Ancestor and said to the Holy Emperor."Go without worry.""Even the Dharma-Transmitting Immortal Ancestor will not be able to kill me in a short time."Tianyang was no longer in the form of a young child, but had transformed into a puppet of dark gold and crimson intertwined.

Roughly the size of an ordinary human."Take this!" The Holy Emperor handed the white ring to Tianyang, while he himself flew away from the Xuanhuang Realm, following the golden needles that were still continuously descending, searching for the Heavenly Doctor's whereabouts.

He also attempted to intercept the golden light midway. But the golden needles came from beyond the heavens, far too fast and far too dense. There was simply no time to stop them. Only by finding and eliminating the source of the golden needles could this be stopped.

Looking at Tianyang, who was already battling the Immortal Ancestor, and noticing no trace of Mo Rubin appearing. The Holy Emperor could not afford to hesitate and once more charged into the starry sky. After entering the void, the insect-transformed spiritual energy rapidly dissipated.

Had the Dao One insects not gained some benefit when the Xuanhuang Realm absorbed immortal spiritual qi and underwent ascension and sublimation earlier, their rate of death would likely have been even faster now. The Holy Emperor understood that he had to find the Heavenly Doctor's trace as quickly as possible.

Unfortunately...This expert, who also seemed to be at the Supreme realm, apparently had no intention of appearing and fighting him directly. He remained hidden the entire time. And the Holy Emperor also discovered that these golden needles seemed as if they had been arranged long in advance.

They were not merely being launched from one location in the starry sky. Rather, from every possible direction, countless golden needles appeared. Falling into the Xuanhuang Realm with perfect precision. The Holy Emperor only felt the frustration of possessing overwhelming martial prowess but having nowhere to use it.

In his mind, the image suddenly appeared of the obliterating immortal calamity sealed outside the Great Qi small world, along with the Great Mage's skeletal remains. He actually faintly developed the thought of breaking the seal and detonating them."Hm?"Very quickly, the Holy Emperor was shocked by his own thoughts and forcibly suppressed them."How could I have such an idea?"The thought flashed past, and the Holy Emperor could only continue searching for the Heavenly Doctor's trace.

As the golden needles continued to fall, the Xuanhuang Realm gradually began returning to its original appearance. Between heaven and earth, the White Mist Barrier rose once more, dividing the various prefectures. The restless Xuanhuang Realm seemed to have been sealed by the golden needles, falling into slumber.

The helplessness in the Holy Emperor's heart had already reached its peak. He casually glanced once more at the Xuanhuang Realm below...And suddenly his heart shook violently. Because he abruptly discovered that the Xuanhuang Realm had not completely returned to its original state.

Rather...That feeling of being within a formation had not faded at all. Instead, as time passed, it became even stronger.
