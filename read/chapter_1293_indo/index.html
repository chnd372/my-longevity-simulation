Bab 1292: Great Qi Soars Above Xuanhuang

Sun Erlang, as the Holy Dynasty's senior disciple, commanded all affairs of the Holy Dynasty in the Holy Emperor's absence. Naturally, no one objected to his pre-battle summary and mobilization. After remaining hidden within the white mist for several more days, a streak of light tore across the sky and flew into the Samantabhadra True Boat.

It was a transmitted message from an unknown source. After narrowing his eyes and checking it, Sun Erlang was visibly invigorated. "We have received confirmed news. The Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance will launch the battle to reclaim Nine Mountains Prefecture tomorrow.

At the same time, in order to intimidate the Five Elders Association, they will also launch small scale harassment campaigns across multiple bordering prefectures..."Upon hearing this, the eyes of everyone in the Holy Dynasty lit up."The Immortal Alliance's move is even bigger than we expected.""Let them fight.

The harder they fight, the better."With a thought from Sun Erlang, an enormous sphere of light representing the territorial map of the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance instantly appeared before everyone."The moment the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance begins the battle to reclaim Nine Mountains, the Holy Dynasty army will begin its campaign.""Before that, none of you are to act rashly."Even as Sun Erlang spoke, he was already coordinating with Ou Shangtian to activate the internal lockdown formation of the Samantabhadra True Boat."Before the decisive battle, there is one top secret matter I must reveal.

It concerns the outcome of this war and absolutely must not be leaked. Therefore, during this period, the war vessel will be sealed. Entry is permitted, departure is not. All communication will also be cut off.""My apologies, everyone," Sun Erlang said calmly.

A trace of surprise flashed through everyone's eyes. They could not understand what kind of secret required such caution. Sun Erlang closed his eyes and waited. In the killing atmosphere, the Samantabhadra True Boat also fell into absolute silence. After an unknown amount of time, Sun Erlang suddenly opened his eyes."The Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance has made its move.""Now, it is our turn."Sun Erlang abruptly stood and bowed deeply toward the empty space ahead.

"Greetings, Master."Everyone aboard the vessel was instantly shocked and looked in the direction of Sun Erlang's bow, only to see the Holy Emperor's figure descend beneath a canopy of starlight."Welcome, Holy Emperor!" everyone called out in unison. Even though it was only a projection and not the Holy Emperor's true body appearing in person, no one dared show the slightest disrespect.

The Holy Emperor did not speak any grand empty words. He merely said in a deep voice, "In this battle, the Holy Dynasty shall be victorious."Then with a wave of his hand, the Holy Emperor's projection transformed into countless points of starlight that flew into the bodies of everyone present.

Boom! Every person felt as though they had transformed into a planet and arrived within a vast star sea. They continuously revolved around the gigantic central star, which resembled the sun in the heavens. Even more marvelous was the immense stellar force echoing throughout the star sea, like a woven net of stars connecting everyone together.

Without needing speech or divine sense, they could communicate freely through mere thoughts. They also discovered that the stars in this sea did not consist only of the people currently within the Samantabhadra True Boat. There were many more, Holy Dynasty grassroots commanders who had already infiltrated various prefectures of the Immortal Alliance."I will now assign deployments."Sun Erlang's voice echoed through the star sea."Wang Xuanba and Senior Qiao Gong will lead the First and Third Legions to besiege the one hundred thirty prefectures of the eastern Immortal Alliance.""Ou Shangtian and Ou Daozi will lead the Second and Ninth Legions to take responsibility for the one hundred twenty six prefectures of the southern Immortal Alliance.""Su Yuqing and Senior Hundred Flowers will lead the Fifth Legion and the Federation of All Worlds allied army to attack the one hundred fifty four prefectures of the western Immortal Alliance."...As Sun Erlang completed each deployment order, Wang Xuanba and the others felt their positions within the star sea rapidly shift.

After settling into entirely new star regions, they simultaneously established connections with the other planets in those sectors. With a slight movement of thought, detailed information about the main commanders under their respective legions appeared in their minds."The operational objective is the capture of each prefecture's Heavenly City.

As for the other cultivation family factions within the prefectures, if they resist, eliminate them in passing. If they choose to observe, they may be ignored for now.""When the war begins, the Tianxuan Mirror channels and teleportation formations across the Immortal Alliance prefectures will all be paralyzed...""This is the defensive grand formation diagram for the Immortal Alliance Heavenly Cities.

Follow the weaknesses marked in the diagram to break them. Their defenses will be as good as nonexistent to us. If backup formations are activated, I will immediately transmit the new methods for breaking them."...As each piece of information was transmitted in orderly fashion, the people present became increasingly excited while reading the materials in their hands.

Not only were there lists of the main cultivators stationed in each prefecture, but their cultivation realms, combat strength, and even cultivation techniques were all clearly marked. Their enemies' secrets had practically been stripped bare. Meanwhile, the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance knew almost nothing about them.

How could this war possibly be lost? No wonder Sun Erlang had been so solemn earlier. The intelligence in their hands could only have been leaked by the highest levels of the Immortal Alliance. But the shock Sun Erlang brought was not over yet."The star sea network you now see is a manifestation of the Holy Emperor's great power.

Aside from basic communication, you may also use the Holy Emperor's power to instantly transfer to one another's locations.""Use this advantage to overwhelm the few with the many and destroy them in divisions."The bright central star in the sea suddenly erupted with dazzling radiance, shining upon all the surrounding stars.

In an instant, detailed methods for using the Holy Emperor's power appeared in everyone's minds. Once again, they could only marvel at the Holy Emperor's greatness....While the pre-battle grand council was still underway, in the Lingxu Realm."There should not be any problems, right?" Xiao Qing and Xiao Hong looked at the wheel-like pattern engraved on the open ground ahead."Please rest assured, seniors.

With me, Cui Shaojiang, taking action, there will definitely be no mistakes," a young man wearing a blue round cap said with a smile. Cui Shaojiang forced out a drop of his essence blood and flicked it toward the open ground. After the blood merged into the wheel pattern on the ground, countless blood colored threads erupted from it.

They then shot rapidly into the sky and disappeared."Ah, in the current Beast Taming Sect, I am the only one who still knows this unique skill." Cui Shaojiang shook his head as he explained. Xiao Qing and Xiao Hong looked somewhat suspiciously at the wheel pattern on the ground.

They had not sensed any restraining or binding force from it. Seeming to notice their distrust, Cui Shaojiang patiently explained further. "The Beast Taming Sect of today is no longer what it once was. After the great calamity, we migrated here. Over the following thousand years, we gradually abandoned the traditional methods of beast control and instead turned to the technique of Beast Spirit Unity."As Cui Shaojiang spoke, the blue round cap atop his head suddenly came alive, sprouting two round black eyes that examined Qing and Hong.

Even though they already knew some details about the Beast Taming Sect, witnessing it personally still left Qing and Hong in disbelief. In their senses, there were not two separate auras of beast and human. From beginning to end, there had only been a single person standing before them."We do not control with techniques, but merge through spirit.

This was the breakthrough our Beast Taming Sect ancestors achieved after researching the Beast Taming Wheel. Speaking of which, Senior Xu Ke also contributed to this." Touching the top of his head, the blue round cap returned to normal."I've wandered off topic.

Returning to the Beast Taming Wheel. The replica Senior Xu Ke sent is indeed excellent. Combined with our Beast Taming Sect's thousand years of accumulated techniques, it is enough to unleash the power of the true Beast Taming Wheel from back then and summon Emperor Sanmo here..."Excitement flashed in Cui Shaojiang's eyes.

"We have long looked forward to this legendary beast of wisdom. If we can merge spirits with it..."His gaze drifted unintentionally beyond the clearing, where the Beast Taming Sect had already laid a heavenly net and earthly snare. The sect had never forgotten the strange beast that abandoned them without a word and left the sect.

If anything, the memory had only grown more unforgettable with time. Thus, when Xiao Qing and Xiao Hong arrived to persuade them with the replica Beast Taming Wheel, both sides immediately agreed. On the ground, the red light moved as though alive, constantly brandishing claws and fangs.

The wheel pattern itself trembled slightly, as if it might leap from the ground at any moment. Cui Shaojiang narrowed his eyes. He could feel that Emperor Sanmo had taken the bait....At the headquarters of the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance. While waiting and pretending to wander around sightseeing, Xu Ke and Yin Shangren suddenly noticed that the Museum of Divine Treasures seemed to become slightly brighter in an instant.

The faint oppressive heaviness weighing on their hearts also suddenly disappeared."Granduncle San has left." Xu Ke immediately made the judgment."Then do we make our move now?" Yin Shangren asked eagerly."Wait a little longer. Wait until the Holy Emperor's side...""The Beast Taming Sect's breakthroughs over these years have surprised even me.

When the great calamity arrived back then, they immediately chose to withdraw and preserve a large number of talented people. That decision truly proved useful," Xu Ke said."Fine, then we wait. A little more time makes no difference. Oh right, come take a look at this..."Yin Shangren pulled Xu Ke in front of an exhibit.

Xu Ke focused his gaze and saw a rather strangely shaped golden tree. Its leaves were all triangular, and the trunk was carved full of tiny figures interacting in all sorts of different poses."This thing is not an ancient artifact of the Xuanhuang Realm, is it?" Xu Ke asked after observing it for a while, his expression somewhat strange."Of course not.

This came from my homeland.""Or rather, from the fragment whose residual consciousness remained the most complete.""I originally thought that place had not even a trace left, yet unexpectedly, the most important Golden Tree was actually preserved in this Museum of Divine Treasures," Yin Shangren said leisurely."This tree...

does it possess some mystery?" Xu Ke knew Yin Shangren would not bring up old memories for no reason, so his heart stirred and he asked. Yin Shangren chuckled. "In our world, there was no sun or moon in the heavens. All the world's light came from this single Golden Tree.

Even the birth of all life came from beneath this tree. You saw those patterns on the trunk. They are the marks left by generations of human interaction beneath the tree.""Although the world was destroyed, as long as this tree remains, it is equivalent to preserving nearly one third of that world's source power."Xu Ke understood somewhat, then said with slight surprise, "You have a way to detonate the energy within this tree?"Yin Shangren rubbed his scalp and smiled without answering."You can bear to do it?

That is your homeland. If this tree remains intact, then after the Holy Emperor captures the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance headquarters, asking him to intervene might not make it impossible to recreate that world," Xu Ke said. Yin Shangren snorted coldly.

"Everyone is already dead. Even if the world is restored, so what? Am I supposed to transform into their appearances and perform a one man play by myself?""If it explodes, then it explodes."Xu Ke responded with a smile. As the two spoke, they suddenly felt the ground beneath their feet tremble slightly.

The two exchanged glances and simultaneously looked beyond the Museum of Divine Treasures. Though their sight could not penetrate through, they could faintly sense that a tremendous upheaval had already descended upon the Xuanhuang Realm. Their senses were not wrong.

At this moment, a wondrous spectacle was unfolding across the skies of the Xuanhuang Realm. The previously bright heavens stretching for thousands of miles suddenly dimmed, as though night had fallen. The great sun in the sky vanished. Specks of starlight lit up across every corner of the heavens.

Amid the shining stars, a bright moon rose. But very quickly, the cultivators of Xuanhuang discovered that this "Bright Moon" was even more luminous than the original sun. As the enormous jade disc slowly approached, the Xuanhuang Realm passed from dimness back into light.

The cultivators raised their heads toward the sky and discovered that within this bright moon stood countless towering structures. It was clearly another world. After a brief moment of mental blankness, countless thoughts instantly surfaced in the minds of the Xuanhuang cultivators."Could this be the legendary Xuan Immortal Ark?""The Xuan Immortal Ark has attacked?""I heard the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance and the Five Elders Association are at war.

Could this be a surprise assault by the Five Elders Association?""Stay alert!"...When something behaves abnormally, there is always something suspicious behind it. With the world currently in a wartime state, the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance's response could not have been faster.

The defensive grand formations of every Heavenly City across the prefectures activated immediately, and alarms echoed through the skies. Even more cultivators transformed into streaks of white light, flying straight toward the bright moon world in the sky, attempting to investigate.

But though it appeared to be within arm's reach, those scouting cultivators could not reach it no matter how they flew. It was as though a heavenly abyss lay between them. The Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance stood ready in full vigilance, only to see several beams of light descend from the moon.

The moonlight was like blades, accurately striking the borders of the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance's four central prefectures: Tianyu, Tianchen, Tianshu, and Tianquan. The skies above the Alliance's four central prefectures instantly seemed coated in a layer of silver radiance, becoming illusory and dreamlike.

But aside from this, there appeared to be no other abnormalities. After waiting cautiously for a long time, the cultivators in the four central prefectures reached this conclusion. However, they soon realized they were wrong."The teleportation formations are unusable.

We have been sealed off.""The communication talismans cannot be used either.""We have lost contact with the other prefectures. But Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance headquarters can still be contacted.""What does headquarters say? Act according to circumstances?

The enemy is already at our doorstep. Damn it."The four prefectures immediately descended into chaos. Some hot tempered cultivators flew directly toward the prefectural borders shrouded in silver light. They attempted to break through the blockade. The white mist barrier under the silver radiance gained an even more sacred and solemn aura.

The cultivators gathered their courage and charged directly into the white mist. They had already prepared themselves to lose years of lifespan, but the scene they witnessed after entering the mist left them utterly dumbfounded. The instant they crossed the border, the white mist filling their vision disappeared.

In its place was a boundless starry sky. It was as though they had truly stepped into the void outside the Xuanhuang Realm, surrounded by darkness and cold. In the distance, countless brilliant stars could faintly be seen flashing from time to time. The cultivators froze in shock and tried to return, only to discover that the path they came from had vanished.

Thinking it was merely an illusion, they unleashed sword arts and spells one after another, yet failed to stir even the slightest ripple in the starry sky. They attempted teleportation and instant movement techniques again, but all failed. Instead, as the spiritual qi within their bodies gradually depleted, the cold starry environment around them slowly began eroding their minds.

The cultivators had no choice but to pick a direction and continue flying at high speed. Trying to leave the bounds of this starry sky. But what they did not know was that the Star Sea was boundless. If the method was wrong, then even exhausting an entire lifetime, they would never be able to leave this place.

Outside the grand formation, cultivators continuously stepped into the moonlight boundary. Yet this formation was like a bottomless abyss, swallowing every single one of them. Nascent Soul, Soul Transformation, even Dao Integration. No matter their cultivation level, once they entered, none ever returned.

After a round of testing, the cultivators of the four prefectures under the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance were ultimately intimidated. The Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance headquarters finally issued an emergency order, forbidding the suicidal actions of the cultivators from the four prefectures.

Several streaks of rainbow light crossed prefectural territories and arrived at the moonlight boundary of Tianquan Prefecture. Among them was none other than Lu Xichan, Chief Formation Master of the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance."This formation..." Lu Xichan examined the moonlight shining upon the white mist barrier, shock surging in her eyes.

Beside her, a young man in white frowned and asked, "How long until it can be broken? Right now, we have completely lost contact with the prefectures outside the grand formation.""It is not only the teleportation formations that have failed. Even the Tianxuan Mirror channels have been blocked." The young man in white spoke with some concern.

This young man in white was one of the true ruling core Dharma-Transmitters of the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance, Dharma-Transmitter [Ren].Lu Xichan did not dare be careless and answered, "This formation seems to draw down the great power of the Star Sea, then uses endless spacetime transformations to generate something akin to a real starry sky..."Dharma-Transmitter Ren coldly interrupted Lu Xichan.

"When can the formation be broken?"Lu Xichan abruptly fell silent. After a long pause, she said, "The complexity of this formation is unlike anything I have seen in my life. At minimum, it should require fifteen days..."Dharma-Transmitter Ren said coldly, "I am giving you ten days."After letting out a cold snort, Dharma-Transmitter Ren flashed away.

Only Lu Xichan and several formation masters from the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance Strategic Formation Hall remained. Lu Xichan raised her head to look at the starlight grand formation covering the sky above, cold sweat slowly appearing on her forehead.

And the higher the formation attainments of the formation masters present, the greater the despair in their eyes. It absolutely could not be said that the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance formation masters were useless. The reality was that the formation the Holy Emperor had deployed this time had far surpassed the current era.

Based upon Li Fan's formation knowledge accumulated over a hundred reincarnations through continuous deductions with the Final Dissociation Disc, knowledge surpassing several generations of the Xuanhuang Realm, and further integrating the insights Li Fan, or rather the Final Dissociation Disc, gained after obtaining several Immortal-grade formations.

After being infused with these formation essences, the Holy Emperor, through his enlightenment within the star sea, pushed the formations to an even deeper level. At this moment, within the Great Qi Holy Dynasty, inside the enormous moon hanging above the skies of the four Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance prefectures and occupying nearly half the heavens.

Countless citizens of Great Qi had all gathered within the Holy Heart Hall. Facing the statue of the Holy Emperor, they knelt and prayed devoutly. And beneath the Holy Emperor Throne in the Holy Capital. There were also tens of thousands of second generation You Clansmen race who had been created, chanting the great merits of the Holy Emperor.

The violet-gold fortune of the Holy Dynasty and the golden source power essence merged into one. Forming faint violet-gold starlight that departed the Holy Dynasty's territory, sustaining the operation of the grand formation trapping the four prefectures of the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance.

This ultimate trapping formation, named the [Bright Moon Starlight Grand Formation] by the Holy Emperor, was almost infinitely close to a true Immortal-grade formation. Li Ping believed that even if a Immortal Ancestor arrived personally, it would still take some time to break through.

As for the people currently inside the four prefectures of the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance trying to break the formation. They would first have to ask whether the Heavenly Mandate Holy Emperor agreed. Li Ping sat upon the Holy Emperor Throne, his gaze crossing the boundary between the two worlds and falling upon the territory of the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance in the Xuanhuang Realm.

The Holy Emperor Throne, or rather the entire Holy Capital, had become incomparably quiet with the great battle having begun. Cat Treasure remained as always, lazily sleeping against Li Ping's shoulder. It seemed that apart from source power essence, nothing in the world could stir its interest.

Aside from Cat Treasure, there was also a figure that appeared to be only eight or nine years old standing beside the Holy Emperor. Staring intently at the Xuanhuang Realm below. It was Tianyang. At this moment, after his puppet body had received massive infusions of source power essence, along with the combat memories of the Immortal Puppet Ancestor, he had already undergone earthshaking changes compared to before.

His appearance and body had returned to how they were in life. He had even genuinely developed the aura of life. His strength was unfathomably profound as he gazed at the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance, waiting for his opponent to descend. And below the Holy Emperor Throne, Daoist Yi Jue of the Du'e Sect had brought out every single paper figure from the entire sect.

The paper figures reconstructed the mountain gate of the Du'e Sect from those bygone years right on the spot. At the center of the buildings, a passage leading to some unknown place seemed to have been opened. Although Daoist Yi Jue himself had not entered it, his eyes bulged outward and extended infinitely, probing deep into the passage."Holy Emperor, rest assured.

If there is the slightest disturbance in the Xuanhuang Realm, this old Daoist will definitely see it," Daoist Yi Jue said with a cheerful smile. Li Ping did not pay Yi Jue any attention. In truth, more than half of his focus and attention was on the starry sky outside the Xuanhuang Realm.

There, he had already made layer upon layer of preparations. Waiting for the Heavenly Doctor and the Immortal Ancestor to descend. Outside the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance Four Prefectures, beyond the Bright Moon Starlight Grand Formation. At the very moment the sky of the Xuanhuang Realm turned incomparably dim, when the Holy Dynasty Great Qi descended with subsidiary small worlds resembling stars, and all the attention of the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance cultivators was drawn away.

A tremendous upheaval simultaneously erupted within the Heavenly Cities of every prefecture of the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance. The moment Sun Luyuan saw the bright moon appear in the sky, it was as if some switch in his mind had been flipped. Without any thought whatsoever, he directly issued orders to those deathsworn soldier he had prepared for many years.

These deathsworn soldier would use their own lives as the price to detonate the formation restrictions left within their bodies inside the prefectural teleportation formations and the Tianxuan Mirrors. Sun Luyuan, who had long since entered Dao Integration and presided over a great cultivation family, actually experienced a momentary blankness in his mind after issuing this order.

His throat went dry, and his hands and feet turned ice cold. Yet before the delayed fear could rise within him, he discovered with some astonishment that in some prefectures, before his deathsworn soldier even had the chance to act, the Tianxuan Mirrors and teleportation formations had already been destroyed by others first.

Sun Luyuan immediately understood."For something this important, it was impossible they would entrust it to me alone."Amid the chaos, Sun Luyuan saw gigantic flying vessels radiating golden light appear abruptly, like divine soldiers descending from heaven.

Before the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance cultivators could even react, the Heavenly Cities had already been surrounded. Strange-looking puppets flew out from the vessels, firing streams of black light. The supposedly impregnable defensive formations of the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance Heavenly Cities instantly became precarious.

As if they could be broken at any moment."It seems the rank of the traitors within the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance may be even higher than mine.""Could it be that even Chief Lu has defected?"Sun Luyuan narrowed his eyes as he watched, immediately feeling far more at ease."The promising younger members of the family have already been arranged by me to leave the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance Four Prefectures in advance.

As for those remaining, I already warned them beforehand. As long as they hide in their cave dwellings, they should not be caught in the disaster.""It is just a pity about Lu'er. I could not transfer him out in advance.""Tianci seems to be in seclusion.

His strength surpasses even mine, so there should be no need to worry about him."As Sun Luyuan thought, he saw the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance beginning to organize cultivators into coordinated resistance. He could not help but let out a cold snort."Now is the time for me to establish merit."With extreme caution, he changed both his appearance and aura.

Then, catching them off guard, Sun Luyuan launched an attack against the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance defensive formations from within. Coordinating with the Holy Dynasty soldiers outside the Heavenly City, they quickly secured victory. As a talisman personally written by the Holy Emperor was affixed to the forehead of the statue of the Immortal Ancestor within the Heavenly City.

It represented the complete fall of that Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance Heavenly City. The first Heavenly City captured by the Holy Dynasty had appeared. Nandu Prefecture. Although this was partly because the prefecture's defender possessed only Soul Transformation cultivation and its defensive forces were exceptionally weak, the significance remained immense.

Very soon, through the Holy Emperor's great power and the Star Sea Net, all soldiers of the Holy Dynasty learned of this matter. The morale of the Holy Dynasty forces was greatly boosted, and they intensified their offensive once again. The battle situation was even more favorable than imagined.

The Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance had enjoyed peace for too long, and most of the combat forces in various regions had already been transferred to the borders. In addition, the defensive formations they relied upon most for counterattacks had all failed.

Thus, in the face of the Holy Dynasty's fierce assault, the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance was almost entirely being beaten one-sidedly. Sun Erlang looked at the territorial map of the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance before him, its colors changing rapidly due to the constant reports of victory, yet there was not the slightest joy in his eyes.

Because he knew the current results only existed because the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance had been caught completely off guard. Once they reacted, the true bitter battle would begin."Liuguang Prefecture, a Dao Integration cultivator has appeared. Blue Ice Immortal Sage.""Yueyang Prefecture has also encountered a Dao Integration enemy.

No information exists on him. Repeating, the enemy's identity is unknown."...Watching the endless stream of battlefield reports and sensing the black-gold puppet in his mind, Sun Erlang pondered briefly and did not act rashly. If they were only ordinary Dao Integration cultivators, the currently allocated military strength would be sufficient to deal with them.

He needed to wait until the true opponent revealed themselves before making his move. Moreover...Sun Erlang's gaze turned toward the area directly beneath the bright moon in the sky, where the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance Four Prefectures were located.

The battles on the outskirts were merely appetizers. The place that would truly determine victory or defeat was there. The Dao Deviation Stone in his mind flashed with blue light as Sun Erlang carried out another deduction, striving to complete the outer battlefield with the fewest casualties possible."Master, I will not disappoint you."Sun Erlang silently said in his heart.

The Holy Dynasty Great Qi advanced victoriously in battle after battle, but this momentum was soon interrupted by an unexpected event. Luoyan Prefecture. After Luoyan Heavenly City was captured, most of the Holy Dynasty soldiers transferred to other battlefields.

Only a small portion remained behind to guard it. But from beyond the Luoyan Prefecture wilderness, a large group of cultivators suddenly emerged. They did not attempt to retake Luoyan Heavenly City, but instead launched a surprise attack and swiftly retreated.
