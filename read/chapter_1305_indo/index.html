Bab 1304: Return to [Truth] Truly Changes

Li Fan originally should have been unable to embark onto the path of cultivation. Back when he traveled from Great Xuan to Liuli Island, merely the hurdle of removing the immortal-mortal miasma within his body had almost completely severed his path to cultivation.

Fortunately, with the dual support of the Xuanhuang Clear Heart Mantra and the Spiritual Pool of Body Purification, he was finally able to begin true cultivation. And at the moment he shed the miasma throughout his body, the Xuanhuang Clear Heart Mantra officially transformed into the Xuanhuang Heart Refining Mantra.

The Xuanhuang Mantra thus became his alternative Dao Foundation, accompanying him throughout his entire cultivation life. As Li Fan's cultivation grew higher, the Xuanhuang Mantra Dao Foundation expanded alongside it. Like pillars, they became an important part supporting Li Fan's cultivation.

And in the previous life, after Li Fan spread the Xuanhuang Immortal Heart Mantra on a large scale, the consequence was that these Dao Foundation pillars...Became weakened because they were divided among too many. His true body's Dao Foundation was not solely the Xuanhuang Immortal Heart Mantra, but also [Truth], as well as the experience and knowledge accumulated through a hundred lifetimes of reincarnation.

Therefore, the impact on him was negligible. But the negative effects caused by this extraction were naturally amplified in clones and divine sense incarnations. The weaker the separated body, the more severe the effect. It was not only Li Fan who was affected by the widespread transmission of the Immortal Heart Mantra.

Even the Immortal Heart Mantra itself became significantly weaker because of this spread. This could be seen from the performance of the Qingxuan Army and the Great Qi Imperial Guard, both of whom cultivated the Immortal Heart Mantra in the previous life, during the great war.

They barely managed to remain calm in crisis, but compared to the orthodox Immortal Heart Mantra's effect of enduring a thousand lifetimes of calamities while the immortal heart remains unchanged, they fell far too short. The difference became even clearer after Li Fan returned to this life through [Truth] and alone monopolized the Immortal Heart Mantra."Techniques cannot be cultivated simultaneously...""It seems this is not merely the Heavenly Dao of the Xuanhuang Realm suppressing abrupt evolution among cultivators.

Bai passed down to the world to offset and reverse the power of the Xuanhuang inverse principle had its origin in the Xuanhuang Great Celestial Venerable. The Xuanhuang Great Celestial Venerable, appointed by the Immortal Realm, wielded immortal artifacts and supervised Xuanhuang.

Within the Xuanhuang Realm, he possessed supreme authority. However, becoming this Great Celestial Venerable of the Immortal Realm was far from easy. Not only was there a colleague known as the Watcher secretly restraining him, but inheriting the office of Great Celestial Venerable also meant inheriting the restrictions and punishments imposed by the Immortal Realm upon the Xuanhuang Great Celestial Venerable.

Only after [Truth] brought him back to this life and Li Fan reviewed matters did he remember Sun Tianci, who had been burned to ashes by heart fire."Even though the Immortal Path is now severed, the Immortal Realm's countermeasures still remain effective.""The Immortal Heart Mantra's inability to spread on a massive scale is likely one of the many restrictions the Immortal Realm imposed upon the Xuanhuang Great Celestial Venerable."Li Fan then recalled what happened after Sun Tianci successfully inherited the office in the previous life."The Great Sun Golden Bell, the Vast Void Azure Lamp, the Purple Qi Dreamweaving Shuttle, the Primordial Five Elements Wheel."These four were the only immortal artifacts remaining among the Xuanhuang Great Celestial Venerable's inheritance.

This was also one reason why Sun Tianci in the previous life was so easily persuaded by Li Fan's divine sense incarnation. Leaving them buried deep within the Fallen Immortal Realm unused would be nothing less than letting treasures gather dust.

In this life, I still must retrieve them. Not to mention that pure mass of immortal spiritual qi."Only after personally using immortal spiritual qi to establish the world extermination immortal formation did Li Fan truly understand the strength and transcendence of immortals.

Within his sea of consciousness, the Final Dissociation Disc emitted unusual seven colored radiance. Within that glow, a true immortal formation was being continuously deduced without pause. Immortal formation birth, immortal formation operation, immortal formation destruction.

During the world extermination process of the All Things Return to Void Formation in the previous life, the Final Dissociation Disc had collected abundant formation data. It was as if it had truly undergone a transformation between immortal and mortal.

Now it could spontaneously deduce immortal formations according to Li Fan's needs, just as it once deduced mortal formations. Li Fan had no means of creating immortal spiritual qi on a large scale. But that did not mean everything within the Xuanhuang Realm had become worthless. So what if beyond the High Wall could hunt True Immortals? Give Li Fan another one hundred and twenty six lifetimes, and perhaps he too could use True Immortals as chess pieces.

Yet this time, merely sensing these so called new interfering variables had caused [Truth] to generously grant an extra reward...In Li Fan's view, this sudden generosity was nothing less than temptation. Guiding and enticing Li Fan to explore beyond the High Wall.

It unquestionably indicated that beyond the High Wall, there absolutely existed objects that [Truth] was extremely interested in, or perhaps found immensely useful. As the fundamental trump card behind all his growth until now, if [Truth] could undergo some kind of change, Li Fan would undoubtedly be the greatest beneficiary.

So how could he not be tempted? If one were to say that during these ten thousand years, with the Immortal Path severed and the High Wall standing tall, some force within the Darkest Star Sea had truly crossed it...Then perhaps there was only...[Shang Immortal Ark].Li Fan's eyes flashed with the shocked expression of the Holy Embryo from the previous life when hearing this matter from Ai Tianjue."They intend to pilot the Shang Immortal Ark and crash into the High Wall, attempting to break out of this star sea." Ai Tianjue's tone was strange.

After the initial shock, the Holy Embryo could not help but ask, "Then did the Shang Immortal Ark succeed?"Ai Tianjue remained silent for a long time before finally speaking with a complicated expression, "If they had not succeeded, why would the Xuan Immortal Ark have treated the words [Shang Immortal Ark] as a taboo for a thousand years?""They...

succeeded?" The Holy Embryo was endlessly shocked, his heart trembling."The Xuan Immortal Ark and Shang Immortal Ark were originally one entity. So the Shang Immortal Ark's escape should be true."There is still a mutual connection between the Xuan Immortal Ark and the Shang Immortal Ark.""If a method to cross the High Wall can be found, then by relying on this connection, one can travel to the Shang Immortal Ark.""With the Shang Immortal Ark as a bridgehead, establishing a foothold beyond the High Wall should become much easier."Because of the original split, the Xuan Immortal Ark possessed no records that could help in crossing the High Wall.

Li Fan urgently needed greater strength to face the Immortal Ancestor, the Heavenly Doctor, True Immortals, and even the forces beyond the High Wall. The battle in the previous life between the Holy Emperor, the Immortal Ancestor, and the Heavenly Doctor had already shown him the path ahead.

Someone like the Holy Emperor, borrowing the fortune of the Holy Dynasty and the favor of heaven, was ultimately inferior to the Immortal Ancestor and the Heavenly Doctor, whose great power belonged wholly to themselves. Cultivating directly within the star sea may not necessarily suit me.""The most practical method..."Li Fan's eyes flickered."Immortal power spreads, Xuanhuang ascends. Merely thinking about it stirred one's blood. During the first three years of this life, whenever Li Fan recalled it, he always felt its mysteries were boundless.

After thousands of years of uninterrupted labor by the Immortal Ancestor and the Heavenly Doctor, the current Xuanhuang Realm could be said to have gathered all the surviving essence of the remnant worlds within the star sea.

Li Fan even sensed faint traces of spiritual energy emerging within heaven and earth, from some unknown source.
