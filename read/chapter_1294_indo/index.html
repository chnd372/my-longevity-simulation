It sent the fleeing Sun family tumbling chaotically. Fortunately, the secret treasure's concealment function was still effective, so they were not exposed.

Sun Liancheng and Sun Qi stared in horror at the valley, which had already turned into a massive circular pit of void. an arrow?"Just as Sun Liancheng stood there dumbfounded, he discovered in horror that several more white arrow lights whistled across the sky. His head instinctively shrank down. He did not even dare breathe, his entire body rigid with tension. Fortunately, after determining that Sun Qi was nothing more than an ordinary mortal with no cultivation, the black tendril spared him. Carrying away the entire Sun family, who had already transformed into the same black flying insects, it howled away into the distance.

Sun Qi still maintained his original stiff posture, not daring to move. Looking at the white mist barrier not far away, as well as the five circular rings in the sky that were gradually fading away. Sun Qi gritted his teeth and began cultivating once more. Not long after closing his eyes to cultivate, Sun Qi suddenly opened them again.

He sharply noticed that the spiritual energy between heaven and earth...Was different from before.
