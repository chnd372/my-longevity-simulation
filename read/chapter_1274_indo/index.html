Bab 1273: The Hometown of the Derivation Law Jade

"Hahaha..."A trace of panic flashed in Derivation Law Jade's eyes as she tried to brush things over."Do not try to muddle through. Tell the truth honestly. Otherwise, I will call someone. Discovering that Yanfa Jue harbors ill intentions would surely count as a great merit.

Perhaps I could even leave early," Li Fan threatened with a half smile."What ill intentions!" Derivation Law Jade glared at Li Fan angrily. However, Li Fan's true body outside the character light sphere suddenly spoke, "Well..."The gazes of the Dao Integration guardians Zhang Xu and Hua Yueye were instantly drawn over.

The little girl Derivation Law Jade immediately panicked."Wait. Let us talk properly!" She rushed forward in one step, standing before the simulated personality, widening her eyes and looking at Li Fan pitifully. Meanwhile, in the Derivation Law Jade space, Li Fan's true body paused slightly before continuing, "Fellow Daoists, I would like to ask.

For techniques derived through applications like Derivation Law Jade, are they allowed to be passed on to others? I know the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance rules prohibit it, but..."Zhang Xu smiled and said, "Fellow Daoist Sun, are you perhaps seeking a technique for your newly born son?

Officially, this is not allowed. However, those responsible for supervision are all from our Guardian Hall. So..."Zhang Xu revealed a knowing look. Li Fan cupped his hands, indicating understanding. Inside the character light sphere, Derivation Law Jade, who overheard the conversation of the three Dao Integration guardians, finally let out a sigh of relief."You..." Derivation Law Jade's expression changed again as she tried to look fierce."Speak.

What exactly do you want?" The simulated personality raised his right hand, bending a finger as if about to flick Derivation Law Jade's forehead. Derivation Law Jade instinctively dodged, but quickly realized that the other party only had a simulated body and no physical form, so she became embarrassed and angry.

However, Li Fan's half smiling expression appeared again, instantly making Derivation Law Jade lose her nerve. In the end, realizing she was no match for Li Fan, Derivation Law Jade admitted defeat."Fine, I will tell you. But you must promise not to report this to the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance," Derivation Law Jade said seriously, dropping her earlier expressions.

Li Fan smiled and replied, "If I were of one mind with the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance, why would I use the opportunity of asking you to derive techniques to pass you the method of simulated personality?"Derivation Law Jade tilted her head and thought for a moment, then realized Li Fan's words made sense.

She clapped her chubby hands, and the scene within the character light sphere suddenly changed. Countless white jade bookshelves stretched endlessly into the sky, filled densely with jade slips. Each jade slip recorded a cultivation technique."At that time, the Heavenly Sovereign ordered me to exhaust all methods in the world.""I was still too young then.

I thought it would be a trivial matter with my abilities, so I happily agreed. Sigh, looking back now, I was truly foolish," Derivation Law Jade said with an old fashioned sigh."I cannot finish deriving them. I simply cannot finish!""So many years have passed, and I am still far from completing the goal.

I feel like I am reaching my limit."Derivation Law Jade scratched her head, looking distressed."I know I must find a way to change all this." A trace of determination suddenly flashed in the little girl's eyes."Oh? And how will you change it?" Li Fan asked with interest as he examined the surrounding techniques."Hmph.

I naturally have my own brilliant plan," Derivation Law Jade said proudly with her hands on her hips."In short, as long as you give me fifty years of free time, I can find a way to escape this suffering in advance. At that time, I will definitely have a good long sleep."Li Fan did not press further and simply repeated, "Fifty years.""Then with this simulated personality helping you now, how long will it take to achieve your goal?"Derivation Law Jade rolled her big eyes and coughed lightly, "I have not tested how efficient this thing is yet.

Why not let me..."Li Fan immediately agreed. After a brief flash of light, everything returned to its previous mechanical state."Master, how may I serve you?" the simulated personality asked dutifully. Derivation Law Jade waved her hand and ordered it to fully take over the derivation work.

The massive character light sphere rotated continuously, and jade slips of techniques formed one after another, appearing on the white jade shelves. Watching these jade slips appear, the joy of effortless gain surged in Derivation Law Jade's heart, making her grin foolishly.

Seven days passed in a flash. Li Fan's voice suddenly sounded again. The moment he took over the simulated personality, the process of deriving techniques was interrupted."Phew...""Phew, phew..."Li Fan looked closely and saw that Derivation Law Jade was floating in the air in an unrestrained posture, sleeping soundly."Mm..." After being awakened by Li Fan, the little girl rubbed her eyes vigorously.

She asked in a daze, "How many years have passed?""Hmm? Only seven days!"When Derivation Law Jade realized the time, she immediately became fully awake."That sleep felt great." She smacked her lips, looking somewhat reluctant."Why are you here again?" She then looked at Li Fan with disdain.

Li Fan did not speak, only stared at her quietly. Derivation Law Jade immediately lost her confidence, "Fine, let me check. Hmm, it is only slightly slower than when I derive with my full effort.""It is barely the same as the speed I usually display in front of the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance."Li Fan nodded slightly, "In other words, after about forty more years, you will succeed?"Yanfa Jue looked confused, "Succeed?

What succeed?""I wanted to escape before because I did not want to work. But now that I have such a good thing working for me for free, why would I run away? Resting every day is not good?"Derivation Law Jade looked at Li Fan with disdain."Oh?""Then if I occupy the simulated personality continuously, how will you respond?" Li Fan said calmly."Hmph, that will not trouble me." Derivation Law Jade suddenly retreated, and the surrounding scene returned to before."Transform!" She pointed toward Li Fan.

As light gathered, another figure slowly formed. It was another simulated personality."A person cannot split their mind to do two things. If you occupy one, can you occupy a second?""Get to work!" Derivation Law Jade said proudly, one hand on her hip while pointing at the second simulated personality."How do you know I cannot?"Unexpectedly, this second simulated personality also spoke in Li Fan's voice.

It looked at Derivation Law Jade with mockery. The expression on Derivation Law Jade's face instantly froze."Fine, fine. I want to see just how many you can control!""Transform. Transform. Transform. Transform..."The little girl form of the Derivation Law Jade kept tapping rapidly in the air, and one after another simulated personality phantoms were continuously generated.

One hundred, five hundred, one thousand. Faces of Li Fan filled the entire interior space of the character light sphere. They spoke in a unified voice, mocking."Not enough.""Is that all?""Is this all you can do?""Continue."As the split simulated personalities increased under Li Fan's taunting words, they had already approached ten thousand in number.

However, a trace of sweat had already appeared on the little girl's forehead. She temporarily stopped, her large eyes staring at the densely packed Li Fan before her, filled with disbelief."You are not human?""What exactly are you?"There was no intent to insult.

In the cognition of the Derivation Law Jade, this was indeed not something an ordinary cultivator could achieve. Occupying a simulated personality was not simply a matter of splitting off a strand of divine sense. It was more like splitting the soul in half and using one part to take control.

To simultaneously control so many simulated personalities was equivalent to chopping the soul into countless pieces while remaining unaffected. She herself relied on the unique ability of a Heavenly Wonder to achieve this. But the other party. A trace of vigilance arose in the Derivation Law Jade's heart."Do you want to continue?"Over ten thousand faces of Li Fan smiled at the same time."Even if there were another hundred thousand, I could still bear it," Li Fan said calmly.

The little girl carefully examined Li Fan's original body within the guarded space of the Derivation Law Jade. He truly showed no signs of pressure. His eyes were closed in contemplation, his expression relaxed. The Derivation Law Jade could not help but hesitate.

It was not that ten thousand had already reached her limit. Rather, she feared that if the two continued competing, it might attract the attention of the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance. Once the number of simulated personalities reached the tens of thousands, it would inevitably place a burden on her normal derivation processes.

And the derivation activity of the Derivation Law Jade was constantly monitored by the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance. After thinking for a moment, the Derivation Law Jade finally compromised helplessly."Fine. I admit you are somewhat impressive."The little girl swallowed the surrounding simulated personalities one by one.

Only the very first one remained. After letting out a satisfied burp, she said, "Speak. What exactly do you want? I can already tell that you definitely have bad intentions."Li Fan shook his head. "You misunderstand me.""I came here to help you regain your freedom."The Derivation Law Jade was unconvinced.

"Freedom? What is so good about freedom? Besides, I have already worked for the Immortal Ancestor for so many years. If I give up halfway, would that not be a waste?""From beginning to end, all I have wanted is to slack off," she reiterated."What benefits did the Immortal Ancestor promise you?" Li Fan asked."Hmph." The Derivation Law Jade refused to answer."Perhaps I can help you as well, and you would not need to pay any price," Li Fan said kindly.

The Derivation Law Jade looked full of disbelief. "Such a good thing exists?""I simply cannot stand seeing others exploited by the Immortal Ancestor," Li Fan said. Her eyes lit up, then she suddenly understood. "I know. You have a grudge against the Immortal Ancestor.

That is why you came to me, to undermine his foundation."Li Fan did not deny it. "You can say that."Upon hearing this, the Derivation Law Jade actually began to think seriously."Hmm. The Immortal Ancestor said he could help me find my home. Can you do that?" she asked tentatively."Home?" Li Fan's gaze flickered.

"Explain in more detail.""Alright." The Derivation Law Jade moved closer and sat down not far beside Li Fan. Resting her chin on her hands, she fell into memory. "Before I was found by the Immortal Ancestor and became the Derivation Law Jade, I was just an ordinary little girl.

But I vaguely retained memories of a previous life.""The scenes in those memories are my true hometown."Sensing Li Fan's gaze, she said helplessly, "I said vaguely. I cannot clearly remember what it looked like. But if I see it with my own eyes, I can definitely tell whether it is real.""I have a feeling that finding information about my hometown is extremely important to me."Li Fan frowned slightly upon hearing this.

Just as he was about to speak, the Derivation Law Jade seemed to have already guessed what he would say. She rolled her eyes at him. "I am not stupid. How could I work for him for so many years based on just empty promises?""He did show me some images of my hometown.

But I cannot display them to you."She gestured vaguely. Li Fan nodded. "I have heard that the Heavenly Wonders of the Xuanhuang Realm are either manifestations of the world's laws or transformations of upper realm immortal artifacts. Could your hometown be the Immortal Realm?"The Derivation Law Jade looked somewhat dejected.

"Who knows. But I do not hope I come from the Immortal Realm. Otherwise, I would have no hope of returning home.""Anyway, can you help me or not?" she asked. Li Fan shook his head. "Before understanding the exact origin of your hometown, I cannot make a conclusion."She looked surprised.

"I thought you would just promise directly and deceive me.""It seems you are not that bad." The little girl reexamined Li Fan. Li Fan smiled faintly. "However, give me some time. I will present all possible scenarios for you to verify one by one.""Really?" She became half convinced."By the way, can you now tell me about your fifty year plan?" Li Fan asked instead of answering directly.

After thinking for a moment, the Derivation Law Jade said softly, "It is nothing special. Just to sleep well for a while and grow up a bit.""After growing up, I will become stronger. Handling these tasks will become easier."
