There was abundant room for cultivation worlds to flourish. At the time, however, he had not paid much attention to it. First, because the great catastrophe had descended and countless cultivation worlds had already been destroyed. The coordinates Sun Piaomiao left behind had therefore become meaningless."'But what if Sun Piaomiao had already foreseen that such a future would arrive, and deliberately left the Medicine King Cauldrons behind to assist in locating them?'"A bold speculation suddenly appeared in Li Fan's mind.

There was no concrete evidence supporting it. It was simply an inspiration that flashed through his thoughts. Meanwhile, the discussion among the Immortal Ark elders continued."The power contained within the Medicine King Cauldron is extraordinarily mysterious.

Perhaps this connection allows one to maintain a sense of direction within the Great Void. If so, then even if exploration ultimately failed, at least one could safely return instead of perishing meaninglessly.""You must understand that the Great Void truly deserves its name.

Exploring the Great Void had once been one of the highest ideals pursued by cultivators of that age. Sun Piaomiao was entirely capable of attempting such a feat."Regardless of his true objective, if we can uncover the mysterious power maintaining the connection between these Medicine King Cauldrons, it will certainly benefit our Immortal Ark."Now that they had a clear direction for their research, progress advanced much more smoothly.

Because they discovered that the material composing the Medicine King Cauldron did not vanish as greater external force was applied.
